# Proposed license scope (draft for owner decision)

This is a technical draft, not legal advice. It does not add or select a license on the owner's behalf.

## Recommendation

If Owen wants a permissive license for files he authored, use MIT **only for clearly identified Owen-authored files**. State explicitly that third-party files retain their own licenses and that files without a clear origin are not included in the grant until reviewed. Do not apply this proposal to JaKooLit-derived GPL-3.0 files, OFL fonts, Catppuccin assets, or other third-party content.

## Draft owner-only text

> Copyright (c) 2026 Owen
>
> Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a copy of the files identified as authored by Owen in this repository (the “Owner Files”), to deal in the Owner Files without restriction, including without limitation the rights to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell copies of the Owner Files, subject to including this notice.
>
> This grant applies only to the Owner Files. Third-party files remain under their respective licenses and notices. Files whose authorship or license is unclear are not included in this grant.
>
> THE OWNER FILES ARE PROVIDED “AS IS”, WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND.

This is intentionally a scope-limited draft, not the standard full MIT text. Before committing a top-level license file, the owner should decide whether this scope is workable and identify the Owner Files in a maintained manifest or per-file headers.
